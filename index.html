Data sources & licences

Fintry Weather Station is a distributor of weather information, powered by data used under open licences. Forecast data is provided by Open-Meteo.com under CC BY 4.0, which combines national weather service models including those of the Danish Meteorological Institute (DMI), the Met Office and NOAA. Rain radar imagery is provided by RainViewer. Basemap © Esri, HERE, Garmin and OpenStreetMap contributors.

This data has been processed and changed by Fintry Weather Station; no endorsement by Open-Meteo, DMI, the Met Office, NOAA or RainViewer is implied. We process this information in a way that is useful to storm chasers, everyday users and severe weather watchers.

Current conditions for Fintry are measured by our own Ecowitt weather station when it is online. Forecasts, and conditions at other places, are computer model output.

Not an official warning service. For official severe weather warnings see the Met Office warnings and SEPA flood alerts. In an emergency call 999. Hill and mountain forecasts: MWIS.
