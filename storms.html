Lightning safety

  • Count the seconds between the flash and the thunder. Divide by 5 for miles (or by 3 for kilometres). Under 30 seconds means the storm is close enough to be dangerous.
  • Go indoors or into a hard-topped vehicle. Stay away from open ground, hilltops, single trees, water and metal fences.
  • Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder before going back outside.
  • Unplug sensitive electronics if a storm is overhead.
